# V12 DESIGN NOTE - SIGNAL / FRAME / MEMORY

## Creative direction
The public interface is treated as a **digital stage**, not a generic portfolio.

- **SIGNAL** = engineering precision, metadata, archive IDs, provenance and rights.
- **FRAME** = cinema, direction, visual rhythm, books, video and photographic composition.
- **MEMORY** = the human purpose of the archive: preserving authored work, music and family history.

The design deliberately avoids a conventional template look. Its visual language combines dark cinema-space, electric cyan signal accents, magenta stage accents, warm editorial paper, grid/circuit geometry and film-strip references.

## Photo archive rule
Photography is a first-class archive, not a subsection of video or biography. Every confirmed photo can receive:
- archive ID;
- title;
- date/year;
- place;
- people/event context;
- source;
- photographer/rightsholder if known;
- rights/publication status.

Unconfirmed identities remain explicitly unconfirmed until the family provides the metadata.
